"Decoding Islamabad’s air quality through data and regression."

This project analyzes 3.5 years of air quality data for Islamabad (2019–2023) to uncover trends in pollutants such as PM2.5, NO₂, and SO₂, and their relationship with environmental factors like temperature and humidity. Using exploratory visualizations, correlation analysis, and regression models, the study identifies seasonal variations, pollutant interactions, and predictive patterns for PM2.5 concentrations.

The Jupyter Notebook provides:

• 	Data exploration: Monthly, yearly, and seasonal pollutant trends.\
• 	Regression modeling: Examining how NO₂, SO₂, temperature, and humidity influence PM2.5.\
• 	Predictive insights: Training regression models to forecast PM2.5 levels.\
• 	Visual storytelling: Clear plots and statistical summaries for easy interpretation.

This case study demonstrates applied data science, statistical modeling, and environmental analytics, making it a strong portfolio piece for showcasing analytical rigor and practical impact.
